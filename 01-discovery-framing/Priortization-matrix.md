## Purpose
With 15 pain points identified, not all can be analyzed with equal depth. This matrix applies objective, weighted criteria to rank pain points and isolate the ones with the highest combined impact.

## Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Question It Answers |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 30% | How often does the problem occur? |
| User Impact | 30% | How strongly does it affect the trainee or other users? |
| Business Impact | 25% | How strongly does it affect bootcamp operations? |
| Urgency | 15% | How quickly does it require attention? |

Each pain point is scored 1–5 on each criterion.

## Process
1. Score each of the 15 pain points across all 4 criteria (1–5 scale)
2. Apply the weighted formula to calculate a final score per pain point
3. Rank all pain points by score
4. Select the Top 5 for deeper root cause analysis
5. Remaining pain points are logged and addressed in later phases
